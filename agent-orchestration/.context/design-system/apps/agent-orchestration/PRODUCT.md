# Agent Orchestration - Product Direction

## Register

product

## Purpose

Agent Orchestration is the governed operator window for one delegated run across
Claude Code, Codex, Grok Build, and Kimi Code. It projects the durable,
hash-chained journal; it never becomes a second source of execution truth.

## Users and promises

- Operators must understand within seconds what is running, where, under which
  provider and permission profile, and whether human action is required.
- Reviewers inspect approvals, evidence, partial results, failures, and cleanup
  obligations without reconstructing them from terminal output.
- Follow-up, approval, rejection, and cancellation show their consequence before
  mutation and preserve provider attribution after it.

## Primary surfaces

- The per-run loopback session UI, with an assistant-style journal at its center.
- Protocol stages and run structure on the left.
- Activity, approvals, and evidence inspectors on the right.
- A governed follow-up composer, connection state, and two-step cancellation.

The product is not a multi-run fleet dashboard, a remote administration surface,
or a source of fabricated cost, token, tool, or provider activity.

## Voice

Exact, calm, and governed. Provider, model, run, workspace, and permission
identifiers remain verbatim. Unverified projection state is labeled rather than
smoothed over.
