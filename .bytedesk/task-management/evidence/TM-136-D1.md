# TM-136 AC7 — closing evidence

Merged at `471c2c3`. AC7 was resolved by **answering** gateway defect D1, not by ruling on whether a
conditional countersignature satisfies a criterion written as "countersigns the addendum".

## Why that was the right resolution

The countersignature is **unconditional** for the load-bearing claim — the six additive keys are
invisible to the real gateway consumer, `schemaVersion` stays `1`, and opening a role vocabulary is
genuinely v2 — and unconditional for five of six keys. It was conditional only on `activity` lacking
an observation time, and the signature itself called that "a one-field fix… free to make now".

Answering it removes the judgment call entirely. There is no longer a conditional signature to
interpret.

## The fix

`activity` gains `observedAt` — when the verdict was last **confirmed** — distinct from `since`,
which is when the state was **entered**. Taken from the census document's own top-level `at`, so it
requires no new observation and is section 5 clean by the same argument as `since`.

The fixtures **show** the census lag (readings confirmed seconds before `generatedAt`) rather than
asserting a coupling between presence cadence and census cadence that deliberately does not exist —
`census.mjs` documents at length why those two clocks must stay independent.
