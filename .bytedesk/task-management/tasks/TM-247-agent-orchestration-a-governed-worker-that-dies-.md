Seen 2026-09-25 on TM-240 and TM-242. Both governed workers exited without a finish report (see TM-246). Recovery then dead-ends: manage start-worker refuses ('Task already has a bound worker; stop it with manage stop-worker first', management.mjs startTaskWorker); manage stop-worker first refused because parking had released the claim, and after re-claiming with tm start refuses because 'Task worker result has not been collected through the finish protocol' (management.mjs:321); manage bind refuses a successor while the dead incarnation is recorded ('Worker incarnation changed', bindTaskWorker). The lead had to fall back to ungoverned tm dispatch, losing the governed review path. The refusals are right for a LIVE worker; for a worker whose process is observed gone, the owner needs a recorded way to retire it.
