## Suite-first positioning

ByteDesk is a family of products for running an AI-powered business: secure access to
your machines, a workforce of AI agents, the tools those agents use, and the platform
that delivers client work. The public site presents ByteDesk as one coherent product
suite, sells the paid tiers, promotes the free products as the way in, and offers
managed services as the done-for-you path on top of the same stack.

## Users

The site serves two audiences at once, and every page must resolve to the right one:

- **Builders (B2C/prosumer, B2B engineering teams)** — developers, operators, and teams
  adopting AI agents. They install free products, buy paid tiers, and self-serve.
- **SMB buyers (B2B)** — owners and operators who want outcomes, not tools. They buy
  managed services delivered through ByteDesk Platform.

## The product family

Three layers, eight products. This taxonomy is the site's information architecture.

**Host layer — own your ground.**
- **ByteDesk Gateway** (free): secure remote access to your own workstation; terminal,
  desktop, files, and an AI assistant behind real auth. The top-of-funnel product.
- **ByteDesk Vault** (paid): fleet identity for teams running many gateways.
- **ByteDesk Store** (infrastructure): the licensing and commerce rail every paid
  product bills through. Not sold; it is how selling works.

**Agent layer — put agents to work.**
- **ByteDesk Workforce** (open-core): the control plane for a company of AI agents;
  org charts, tasks, budgets, approvals. Free community self-host, paid team/enterprise.
- **ByteDesk Agent Browser** (free dev tier + paid scale): the cloud browser agents
  drive; API-compatible with Anchor Browser, self-hostable, OS-level control.
- **ByteDesk Agent Memory** (paid): tiered, permissioned shared memory for agent teams.

**Delivery layer — ship the work.**
- **ByteDesk Platform** (flagship B2B): the SaaS delivery engine; CRM, web intelligence,
  AI workflows. Sold as managed-service engagements and tenant platform.
- **ByteDesk Capture** (freemium desktop): screen capture with annotation, OCR, and
  evidence packages that plug into the rest of the suite.

## Primary CTAs

Per audience, in this order:

- **Builders:** *Install free* (the one-liner installer is the conversion moment), then
  *Buy* (checkout through ByteDesk Store).
- **SMB buyers:** *Book a Growth Consult*.

Every product page ends in the right CTA for its audience. Free products never
dead-end; they route to the installer.

## Better together

Cross-product scenarios are a first-class content type: concrete, step-by-step "in
action" walkthroughs showing products enhancing each other — an agent researching in
Agent Browser, remembering in Agent Memory, coordinated by Workforce, hosted behind
Gateway, licensed through Store. Each scenario is honestly labeled: works today, needs
wiring, or roadmap. Never imply shipped integration that does not exist.

## Proof policy

Non-negotiable. **Product-proof only**: real product surfaces, real workflows, verified
metrics, named case studies with approval. Banned: fake customer logos, fabricated
testimonials, invented growth metrics, "trusted by" strips, placeholder avatar quotes.
Honest maturity labels on every product — a v0.x product says so. Scene photography is
allowed as art direction and must never masquerade as product proof.

## Messaging hierarchy

The first screen answers: what is ByteDesk (an operating suite for AI-powered
business), who is it for (builders and SMB operators), what next (install free / see
the suite / book a consult).

- Headline: the suite promise.
- Subhead: eight products, three layers, one design system, one installer.
- Primary CTA: *Install free*. Secondary: *Explore products*. Tertiary: *Book a Growth
  Consult*.
- Proof area: real product surfaces and the install one-liner itself.
- Product stack section: the three layers with the eight products.
- Scenarios section: better-together walkthroughs.
- Services section: the managed path for SMB buyers.

## Voice

Direct, capable, operational. Plain language, specific outcomes, real artifacts. Honest
about maturity: "v0.9, self-host, single-operator hardened" beats "enterprise ready."
Free means free, not trial.

Avoid: "unlock your potential", "revolutionary all-in-one platform", "AI-powered
transformation", "trusted by industry leaders".

## Success criteria

A builder lands, understands the suite in one scroll, and runs the installer inside
five minutes. An SMB buyer lands, understands ByteDesk can run their growth systems,
and books a consult. Neither audience sees fake proof, and every claim traces to a real
repo, product surface, or shipped artifact.
